# Class Distribution

| ID | Component Name | Responsibilities | Included Classes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **CO1** | Identity and Employee Records | Authenticates users, enforces role-based permissions and the 15-minute inactivity timeout, stores employee, department and emergency-contact records, applies HR-verified profile changes, provides directory search and lets the System Administrator manage accounts, roles and permissions. | C01 Employee, C06 SystemAdministrator, C07 Department, C08 EmergencyContact, C09 UserAccount, C10 Role, C11 Permission, C12 Session, C13 EmployeeDirectory (9 classes) |
| **CO2** | Notifications and Announcements | Creates notifications that identify their triggering request, announcement or event, tracks read status, stores HR announcements with their audience and publication details, and broadcasts each published announcement to the targeted employees. | C39 Announcement, C40 Notification, C41 NotificationService (3 classes) |
| **CO3** | Requests, Approvals and IT Services | Owns the shared request lifecycle (submit, validate, route, status, audit trail and attachments), leave balances and leave decisions, profile-change requests, approval decisions, IT asset borrowing, extension and return, and IT support tickets with their updates. | C17 Request, C18 LeaveRequest, C19 LeaveBalance, C20 ApprovalDecision, C22 AuditEntry, C21 Attachment, C23 ProfileChangeRequest, C02 Manager, C31 AssetRequest, C30 AssetLoan, C29 ITAsset, C32 ITSupportTicket, C33 TicketUpdate, C05 ITStaff (14 classes) |
| **CO4** | Company Content and ONEAI | Stores approved company documents, checks role-based access before search, view or download, answers policy questions only from approved accessible documents (with sources or a no-answer response), escalates to IT only after the employee confirms, and lets HR publish documents and announcements and verify profile changes. | C34 Document, C35 DocumentCenter, C36 ONEAIAssistant, C37 AIQuery, C38 AIResponse, C03 HROfficer (6 classes) |
| **CO5** | Attendance and Dashboard | Imports entry/exit records from the external Attendance System, computes weekly attendance summaries and assembles the personalized dashboard (attendance, pending requests, upcoming leave, unread notifications and latest announcements). | C16 Dashboard, C15 AttendanceSummary, C14 AttendanceRecord, C43 AttendanceAdapter (4 classes) |
| **CO6** | Expenses, Payroll and ERP Integration | Handles travel expense claims and their items, payslip discrepancy reports and Finance review, retrieves payslips from the external ERP (5-second timeout), keeps an authorized payslip cache no older than 24 hours and provides the shared integration base used by all external adapters. | C24 ExpenseClaim, C25 ExpenseItem, C28 PayslipDiscrepancyReport, C04 FinanceOfficer, C26 Payslip, C27 PayslipLineItem, C44 PayrollAdapter, C42 IntegrationAdapter (8 classes) |
