Project Risks and Risk Management



| Risk ID | Risk Description | Possible Cause | Probability | Impact | Mitigation / Response Strategy |

\--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

| R1 | ERP integration problems | Limited API access or differences between ERP systems | High | High | Identify integration requirements early and use mock APIs/data during development. |

| R2 | Security or privacy issues | The system handles employee and company information | Medium | High | Use authentication, role-based access, authorization controls, and restrict access to sensitive information. |

| R3 | Changing requirements | Employees or management request additional features | High | Medium | Define the initial scope clearly and prioritize changes based on importance and available time. |

| R4 | Lack of required technical skills | Limited experience with ERP integration or AI systems | Medium | Medium | Divide responsibilities according to team skills and learn required technologies during development. |

| R5 | Schedule delays | Development or integration takes longer than expected | Medium | High | Develop incrementally, prioritize core functionality, and monitor progress regularly. |

| R6 | ONEAI provides incorrect information | Incomplete or outdated company data | Medium | High | Restrict ONEAI to approved company sources and test responses against known information. |

| R7 | Attendance integration unavailable | Fingerprint/attendance system does not provide suitable access | Medium | High | Use simulated attendance data for the prototype and design the integration layer for future connection. |

| R8 | Team member availability | Academic workload or conflicting schedules | Medium | Medium | Divide work clearly and maintain shared documentation and GitHub contributions. |

| R9 | Poor user adoption | Employees find the application difficult to use | Low | Medium | Use a simple interface and obtain feedback from potential users during development. |

| R10 | Module integration errors | Modules are developed separately | Medium | Medium | Define interfaces early and perform integration and regression testing after each increment. |
